I'm Marcelo Masahiko Miyake, a **Software Engineering Specialist at Atech and Systems Architect**, based in São Paulo, Brazil. I have more than 20 years of software engineering experience, including over 13 years at Atech working on mission-critical systems. I work in English and Portuguese.

My work connects hands-on development, architectural decisions, and technical leadership. I focus on distributed systems, reliability, and practical tooling for AI-assisted development.

## Professional experience

- **Air traffic management:** led full-stack development of a new interface using Spring Boot and Vue.js, and developed control and simulation training systems with C, Java, and DDS under ICAO standards.
- **Cloud and defense:** designed GCP cloud infrastructure in Go for Embraer's Digital Defense project.
- **Avionics:** collaborated with Airbus engineers on C++ embedded software for the EC725 helicopter under DO-178B requirements.
- **Banking and payments:** earlier work includes ATM and PIN Pad software, ISO 8583 transaction processing, and network management systems.

My [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marcelomiyake/) contains my employment history and education. I hold an MBA in Business Management from FGV, have spoken at The Developers Conference about coding standards for critical systems, and placed second in the 2019 NASA International Space Apps Challenge in São Paulo.

## Engineering approach

My focus is the connection between business behavior and the software that implements it: clear requirements, architectural boundaries, application code, and the infrastructure needed to run it. Concurrency, data ownership, and failure handling are part of that design.

In my public AI development experiments, I examine how specifications, context, and feedback loops guide coding agents, then check the behavior they produce. I document decisions and verification limits so readers can reproduce findings and assess the trade-offs.
